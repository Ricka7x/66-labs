Boomark

a bookmark manager for macOS & iPhone

Browser bookmarks are where links go to die. Boomark puts yours behind a hotkey: save, tag and find them without touching the mouse.

The short version

Boomark is a bookmark manager that acts like a launcher. Hit a hotkey, paste a URL or grab the tab you're already on, tag it, and get back to work. When you need it again, it's three keystrokes away, on your Mac and on your iPhone.

7 things it does really well.

01 / 07

A palette, not a folder graveyard

Search, add, open, pin, tag and delete, all from one keyboard-first palette.

02 / 07

Grab the tab you're on

One shortcut saves the frontmost tab from Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge, free, no Pro required. No window switching.

03 / 07

Save from the browser toolbar too

A free browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Arc, Brave and Safari saves without leaving the tab.

04 / 07

Bring your old bookmarks

One-time import from Safari and Chromium browsers. Your folders become tags.

05 / 07

Tags that don't multiply

Case-insensitive with autocomplete, so “Work” and “work” never become two tags.

06 / 07

⌘1–⌘9 quick-open

Your top results, one keystroke each. Faster than typing the URL.

07 / 07

Dress it up

Preset themes with vibrancy, light and dark, or bring your own.
