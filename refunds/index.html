0114-day money-back guarantee

If a paid license (such as a Pro license) isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund it in full. You don't need to explain why, although we'd love to know what we could do better.

Our apps are free to download, and the free version is there so you can try an app before paying. We encourage you to do that first.

02How to ask

Email hello@66labs.dev from the address you bought with, and include your order number or license key. Your receipt from Lemon Squeezy has the order number.

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record for our orders, so the refund is issued by them to your original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it. When a refund is issued, the license key is deactivated.

03After 14 days

We can still help. If an app doesn't work as described on its page, or a bug stops you from using something you paid for and we can't fix it, write to us and we'll make it right, including a refund where that's fair. These cases are decided one by one.

04Your legal rights

This policy adds to the rights you have by law and doesn't replace them. For example, many countries give consumers a right to cancel a distance purchase, or rights when a digital product is faulty. Where such a right applies to you, you keep it.

If you're in the EU or UK, you can cancel a purchase within 14 days of buying. If you start using the license right away, the checkout may ask you to agree to immediate delivery and to acknowledge that the statutory cancellation right can end once delivery starts. Our own 14-day guarantee above still applies either way.

05Please ask us first

A refund request to us is faster than a dispute with your bank, and it means we can fix the problem. Fraudulent or abusive requests, such as repeated refunds of the same license, may be refused.

06Contact

Questions? Email hello@66labs.dev. See also our Terms and Privacy Policy.
